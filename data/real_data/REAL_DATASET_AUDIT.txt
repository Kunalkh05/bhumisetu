5. UNIQUE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Case Identifier Analysis:
1. Official Case / LAC Number:
   - In Maharashtra Revenue Administration, every Land Acquisition Officer (LAO)
     or Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) assigns a case number using the standard
     format: [Case_Serial]/A-65/[Financial_Year] (e.g., 4/A-65/2025-2026,
     9/A-65/2021-2022).
   - Infrastructure projects use project-specific case codes: Case No - 07-2025,
     Case No.16-2025, S.R. 28/2019, 2026/LAQ/KARYA-1/RR-1233/2026.
   - Presence in Data: 140 of 278 cases (50.4%) in real_land_acquisition_cases.csv
     and 205 of 344 events (59.6%) in real_land_acquisition_events.csv have an
     extracted official case number. The remaining 138 represent scanned Marathi
     notices or general gazettes where regex did not detect a case string.
2. The True Statutory Acquisition Unit:
   - A single major public project receives an overarching project serial, but
     the statutory declaration, hearing, and award occur village by village
     (Mouza by Mouza).
   - Therefore, the unique statutory acquisition unit is:
     Unique Case = (District, Taluka, Mouza/Village, Case Number)
3. Can the same case be followed across multiple documents?
   - YES. 31 cases in real_land_acquisition_cases.csv cluster between 2 and 7
     documents.
   - When grouped strictly by (District, Village, Case Number), there are 32
     distinct groups with >= 2 documents, and 17 groups with >= 3 sequential
     statutory documents (Section 11 -> Section 19 -> Section 21).
